About the Ethekwini Eisteddfod

A celebration of musical learning, performance and achievement for instrumentalists and singers of all ages.

Our Purpose

A platform for musicians to grow

The Ethekwini Eisteddfod encourages active and competitive music-making at every level. It gives young and adult amateur performers the opportunity to perform in public, receive professional assessment and develop confidence through music.

Participants are welcomed across classical and contemporary disciplines, from beginners taking their first steps on stage to experienced performers preparing advanced programmes.
